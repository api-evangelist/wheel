# Wheel

> Wheel Health, Inc. is a US telehealth infrastructure company. Its Wheel Horizon
> platform is a modular care-enablement system combining a nationwide clinician
> network, evidence-based virtual care programs, and integrated clinical
> operations, sold to digital health companies, health plans, retailers and
> pharmacies, and pharma/life-sciences organizations. Wheel also runs WheelX, an
> enterprise AI health exchange, and Connected Services integrations for
> prescriptions, pharmacy delivery, labs, insurance, patient payments and remote
> patient monitoring. A "Wheel API" exists and is governed by published API Terms
> of Use, but its reference documentation is not public.

## Agent-relevant facts

- There is NO public machine-readable contract for the Wheel API: no OpenAPI,
  no Swagger, no GraphQL SDL, no AsyncAPI, no Postman collection, no MCP server,
  and no A2A agent card on any Wheel-controlled host (probed 2026-08-15).
- The developer portal at https://developers.wheel.com/ is password-protected at
  the HTTP layer and returns 401 for every path, including /.well-known/*.
- API access requires organizational registration and Wheel approval before
  Access Credentials (keys, secrets, tokens) are issued — Wheel API Terms §3.
- PHI moving through the API requires a Business Associate Agreement — §6.
- Cached Wheel Content may be retained no longer than 24 hours — §7.
- Rate limits exist but no numbers, windows or response headers are published — §10.
- Wheel may change, suspend, or discontinue any part of the API at any time,
  with no notice window and no Sunset/Deprecation header commitment — §10.
- Wheel Content may not be used to train or inform machine learning, and may not
  be sold, leased, sublicensed or transferred to third parties — §5.
- Scraping or crawling Wheel services by automated means other than the Wheel
  API is prohibited — §5. Agents should treat wheel.com as read-only reference.
